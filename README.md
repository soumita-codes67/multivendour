# Fixzo - Hyper-Local Multi-Vendor Home Services Marketplace

A high-performance React application built using **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Plus Jakarta Sans**, translating the multi-page designs from `stitch_organized_multi_page_design` into a cohesive, interactive web application.

---

## 🎨 Design System Implementation (`warm_neighborly_craft`)

- **Palette & Atmosphere:**
  - **Surface Canvas (`#faf9f6`):** Natural artisanal cream replacing cold whites to eliminate digital glare and establish tactile warmth.
  - **Primary Heritage Forest (`#012d1d` / `#1b4332`):** Delivers quiet authority, verified safety markers, headers, and footer anchoring.
  - **Secondary Terracotta & Coral (`#a13e28` / `#e06d53`):** High-urgency conversion triggers and instant booking CTAs.
  - **Soft Mint & Sage (`#c1ecd4` / `#e8f0eb`):** Aadhaar-verified status chips, local coverage pills, and bio-safe chemical certifications.
- **Typography:** Fully integrated Google Font **Plus Jakarta Sans** with tabular numbers and snug tracking.
- **Iconography:** Google Material Symbols Outlined.

---

## 📱 Pages & Features

### 1. **Services Directory (`fixzo_services_directory_1 & 2`)**
- Locality selector for Bengaluru (Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR Layout, Whitefield, Jayanagar, Malleshwaram).
- Live search bar with instant query filtering and clear button.
- Quick category chips: *Cleaning & Hygiene*, *Electrical*, *Plumbing*, *AC & Appliances*, *Painting & Wall Care*.
- Rich service cards with ratings, review counts, checklists, pricing, and "Select Service" toggles.
- Real-time sticky order drawer on the right computing package subtotal, safety fee, Prime discount, and the 40% refundable domestic advance split.

### 2. **Pro Profile & Slot Booking (`fixzo_pro_profile_booking_1 & 2`)**
- Verified Specialist Dossier featuring Priya Sundaram (and other certified pros).
- Verification badges: *Aadhaar Verified*, *Skill Certified Pro*, *Bio-Safe Chemicals*.
- Performance metrics: *100% On-time rate*, *2.4 km distance*, *4+ yrs field experience*.
- Dynamic package selector (1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK Villa, Hourly Pro support).
- Customer testimonials and equipment specifications (industrial vacuum, HEPA steamers).
- Interactive slot picker with Day Selector (Today, Tomorrow, etc.), Time Slots (Morning, Afternoon, Evening), and Instant Dispatch (~25 mins) toggle.

### 3. **Instant Checkout (`fixzo_modern_instant_checkout & slot_confirmation`)**
- 3-step progress bar (Step 1: Service → Step 2: Schedule → Step 3: Payment).
- Escrow protection & 256-Bit SSL security badge.
- Order recap with editable doorstep address in Bengaluru.
- 40% Refundable Domestic Advance breakdown (40% advance held in escrow, 60% balance payable only after 100% customer satisfaction).
- Payment methods: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Custom UPI ID), Credit/Debit Cards, Fixzo Wallet balance, and Pay After Service.
- Interactive promo coupon code support (`FIRSTFIX`, `FESTIVE300`).

### 4. **Slot Confirmation & Live Dispatch Tracker**
- Confetti celebration on booking confirmation.
- Unique booking reference code (e.g., `#FXZ-84920`).
- Live technician tracking simulation with moving route indicator and countdown ETA (~22 mins).
- Doorstep arrival verification 4-digit OTP (`4920`).
- One-click Pro call/chat, Add to Calendar, and Tax Invoice download.

### 5. **Partner Onboarding ("Switch to Provider Mode / Earn")**
- Header toggle for service professionals to join Fixzo.
- Value proposition: *"Earn up to ₹45,000/mo on your own schedule"*, zero joining fee, 100% retained tips, daily 6:00 PM bank transfer.
- Real partner testimonial callout (Manjunath Swamy, Electrician).
- Fast-track 2-minute registration form with instant submission confirmation.

### 6. **Additional Features**
- **My Bookings:** Tab to review active en-route bookings and completed past bookings.
- **Wallet & Offers:** Balance manager, Prime perks overview, and 1-click coupon application.
- **AI Helpdesk:** Diagnostic interactive modal to troubleshoot electrical sparks, pipe leaks, or AC faults with automated safety tips and instant recommended booking.
